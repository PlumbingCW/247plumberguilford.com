About this website

This site gives general information about plumbing problems and services in Guilford, Connecticut and nearby towns, and it gives you a phone number for 24/7 emergency dispatch. It does not take online bookings or payments.

Everything on these pages is general guidance. It is not a diagnosis of your plumbing, and it does not replace an inspection by a licensed plumber who can see the problem.

Information on the site

We work to keep the site accurate, but plumbing codes, utility rules and local requirements change. Before you start work, check current requirements with the town building department or your water and sewer provider.

Do not use this site as a substitute for emergency services. If anyone is in danger, if you smell gas or if there is a fire, leave the building and call 911 first.
