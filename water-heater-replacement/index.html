Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Guilford, CT

When a tank has rusted through, the water heater cannot be repaired and every hour matters. Our 24/7 line sends a plumber for emergency water heater replacement in Guilford, CT. Close the cold supply valve above the tank, turn off the power or fuel to the heater and call.

Replacement is also the right answer when an old heater has been repaired once too often. The pages below explain how that decision is made and what a replacement visit includes.

When a failed water heater is an emergency

A tank that is leaking into a finished space, a heater that smells of gas, or no hot water on a freezing night is an emergency. A slow weep at a fitting or a heater that is simply old is not, and it can wait for a scheduled visit. If you are unsure which you have, call and describe what you see, and the dispatcher will say how to treat it.

What a same-day replacement needs from you

A same-day replacement works when the plumber can bring the right unit. That depends on a few answers you can give on the phone: the fuel, the tank size on the data plate, the location and the type of vent. A closet unit and a basement unit are different jobs, and so are a gas heater with a chimney flue and one with a power vent.

The dispatcher also asks about access, such as basement stairs or a tight attic hatch, because carrying a tank and the old one out can decide the time needed. Photograph the heater, its data plate and the area around it if you can. A plumber who sees the photos can plan parts before arriving.

What a proper replacement includes

These are standard parts of the job. Ask the plumber to confirm each.

  • ✓Draining and removing the old tank. Including disconnecting the fuel, water and vent connections safely.
  • ✓A new relief valve and discharge pipe. The valve must discharge to a safe location, not capped, as the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) that the state adopts requires.
  • ✓New connectors and shutoff valves. Old connectors and corroded valves are replaced rather than reused.
  • ✓Venting checked or rebuilt. Slope, size and clearances matter for combustion appliances.
  • ✓An expansion tank where required. Closed systems need somewhere for expanding water to go.
  • ✓Testing before the plumber leaves. Leaks, flame, venting and temperature are all checked.

Permits, licenses and Guilford's rules

The Guilford Building Department takes permit applications online and says it requires a valid state license and liability insurance at the time of application. It lists plumbing and mechanical work as separate permit types. A replacement of a water heater is not a simple leak repair, so ask the plumber which permit applies and who files it.

The 2022 Connecticut State Building Code adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), but it does not adopt the International Fuel Gas Code, and gas piping and appliances follow other standards. For a homeowner, the point is simple: heaters that burn fuel need correct venting and a licensed installer. You can check any contractor's Connecticut license on the state's license lookup.

Choosing the new heater

A standard tank is the fastest to install and the least costly to buy. A tankless unit saves floor space and supplies hot water continuously, but it needs a larger gas line or heavy electrical service and costs more. A heat pump water heater uses electricity efficiently, though it needs space and airflow. If you are leaning toward one of those, read our tankless page and the notes on planned installation.

Size matters. A tank that is too small runs out. One that is too large wastes energy keeping water hot. The plumber sizes the heater to the number of people, bathrooms and fixtures that run together.

Flood-prone basements and fuel tanks

Guilford has coastal and river flood zones. The town's Engineering Department says work on structures in the Special Flood Hazard Area, which includes FEMA zones A, AE and VE, needs a Flood Hazard Permit in many cases, and fuel tanks are listed among the work covered. Its page also says work that needs only a mechanical permit does not require that permit.

In a basement that floods, the heater is a candidate for raising off the floor. The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration's storm records say Tropical Storm Irene put two to three feet of water on the roadway and properties around Water and River Streets beside the West River in 2011. If your home is in that kind of area, ask the plumber to talk through placement before the new unit is set.

What affects the price of a water heater replacement

A clear price comes before work starts. These items decide it.

  • The heater.Standard, tankless and heat pump units cost very different amounts.
  • Venting and fuel connections.A reusable flue is cheaper than a new vent.
  • Code items.Expansion tank, pan, and valves are part of doing it right.
  • Permit and inspection.A replacement may need a permit, and the plumber files it.
  • The hour.Emergency visits cost more than scheduled ones.

Insurance usually covers sudden water damage from a failed tank, and not the heater itself. Keep the old tank until your adjuster says it is not needed.
