Emergency Faucet Repair in Guilford, CT

When a faucet will not shut off, a supply valve bursts under the sink or a shower valve keeps running, you need the water stopped first and the repair second. Our 24/7 line sends a plumber for emergency faucet and valve repair in Guilford, CT, and the dispatcher can talk you through finding the right shutoff while the visit is arranged.

Most faucet calls are a worn cartridge, a failed washer or a corroded valve. A few are the start of something bigger behind the wall.

First, stop the water

Look under the sink for two small valves, one for hot and one for cold. Turn each clockwise until it stops. If a valve spins freely, drips or will not move, the valve itself has failed, and the next step is the main water shutoff for the house, usually near where the line enters the basement or utility room.

Do this before you do anything else, then call. A supply line that has split can move gallons a minute, and a cabinet floor holds very little.

Which faucet problem do you have?

Dripping spout

A steady drip from the spout means the part that closes the water path has worn. In a compression faucet that is a rubber washer. In cartridge, ball and ceramic disc designs it is the cartridge or seats. Replacement parts are matched to the brand and model, which is why we ask for a photo of the faucet or the name stamped on it.

Leak at the handle or base

Water seeping around the handle comes from worn O-rings or a loose packing nut. Water pooling at the base of a kitchen faucet may come from the hose connections underneath. Both are small repairs that turn into a stained, swollen cabinet if left.

Low flow or sputtering

The aerator on the spout clogs with mineral flakes and sediment. If the whole house is low, the cause may be upstream, and you should ask about our water line and pressure diagnosis.

Supply valves and broken stops

The small valves under sinks and behind toilets are called stops. They sit unused for years and then crack or seize on the day they are needed. A broken stop is one of the most common reasons a plumber is called out at night, because the only way to stop the water is the main valve.

Replacing a stop is a short job when the pipe behind it is sound. If the pipe is corroded, we replace a small section so the new valve has something solid to attach to. That is also the moment to swap old rubber or braided lines that are past their service life.

Shower and tub valve repair

Shower valves are different from sink faucets because most of the working parts are inside the wall. Call if you notice any of these.

  • 1Water too hot or cold. A worn pressure-balancing cartridge can no longer blend the supply properly, which is a scald risk for children and older family members.
  • 2Handle won't shut off completely. The stem or seat is worn. Constant drip from the showerhead is wasted water.
  • 3Moisture on the wall or ceiling near the valve. This can mean a leak behind the wall. Do not wait. The wall can hold water for weeks before the stain appears.
  • 4Tub spout diverter fails. Water runs from the spout when the shower is on. A diverter or spout replacement is usually quick.

Older houses in Guilford and what we find behind faucets

The town center around the Guilford Green was settled in 1639, and the historic district the National Park Service describes includes houses from every century since. Older houses often carry mixed plumbing: new fixtures on old galvanized or copper lines. When a faucet is replaced, we check whether the supply lines behind it are rusting from the inside, because a new faucet on failing pipe only moves the leak.

If a home has a persistent low-flow problem, we may point you to a repiping assessment rather than keep replacing fixtures.

What affects the price of faucet and valve repair

A faucet fix is priced by how deep the problem goes, from a worn cartridge to a corroded valve inside a wall, and you get a clear price before work starts.

  • Fixture type and brand.Parts for common brands are easy to find. Specialty or imported faucets can mean a wait.
  • Repair or replace.A faucet with a repairable cartridge costs less to fix than to replace. A faucet with a corroded body is better replaced.
  • Behind the wall.Tub and shower valves can need an access panel or wall repair.
  • Shutoff condition.If the stop valve is seized, replacing it is part of the job.
  • When you call.Evenings, weekends and holidays are quoted as emergency visits.

A sudden burst is generally covered by homeowners insurance, while a slow drip over months generally is not. Take photos and keep receipts. For your real number, call the 24/7 line.
