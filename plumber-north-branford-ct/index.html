Emergency Plumber North Branford CT Near Guilford

North Branford sits inland, north of Branford and west of Guilford, and our 24/7 emergency plumber line dispatches there around the clock. A burst pipe, a basement taking on water, no water from the tap: call and describe it.

The town has two ZIP codes, 06471 for North Branford and 06472 for Northford, and a water and sewer setup that differs sharply from the shoreline. Most of the town drinks from private wells, and about two thirds of developed properties are on septic.

Water supply: a small public system and a lot of wells

North Branford's plan of conservation and development says the south end and northwest corner of town are served by the Regional Water Authority. Two small community systems serve other parts, and "the remainder of Town relies on wells." The Regional Water Authority also owns about one third of the town's land as reservoir watershed around Lake Gaillard.

For a home on a private well, "no water" is a plumbing emergency with an extra suspect. The cause may be a tripped breaker for the pump, a failed pressure switch, a waterlogged pressure tank or a pump that has given out. A plumber can work through those in order. During the 2020 drought, the town's hazard plan annex says private wells were reportedly drying up, which is worth knowing if a well runs low in a dry summer.

Every North Branford ZIP code, 06471 and 06472, includes both kinds of homes, so the dispatcher asks about water supply and drainage early in the call.

Where the sewer ends along Route 80

About 35 percent of North Branford's developed properties are served by the town sewer, according to the town's Engineering Department. The sewer comes in three separate systems that send waste to Branford, East Haven and North Haven under agreements with those towns. The town's plan says infrastructure runs east along Route 80 as far as Brookmeade Court, and everything past that point on the eastern end of the Route 80 corridor uses on-site septic systems.

If a backup happens in a sewered area and you are not sure whether the clog is in your line or the town main, the town publishes an after-hours sewer backup line at 866-266-4059. Call it if water is coming up and the neighbors are affected too. We handle everything inside your property line, and our camera inspection shows where the problem sits.

Inland flooding and basements

North Branford's 2023 hazard plan annex calls inland flooding the greatest concern. It names the Farm River at Foxon Road, and Harrison Road, Lea Road and Circle Drive near the Branford River and Munger Brook, where flooding from the Lake Gaillard spillway affects homes every 10 to 20 years. The annex says those homes are pre-FIRM, built in the 1950s and 1960s.

A sump pump with battery backup is the first line of defense in those basements. If your house is in a flood-prone spot, ask us about adding a backup unit before the next storm, rather than after the water has risen.

Northford, North Branford Center and the roads between

The town centers on two villages, Northford Center and North Branford Center, each listed as a National Register historic district. The main roads are Routes 80, 17, 22 and 139, and Town Hall is on Foxon Road. Totoket Mountain dominates much of the town, and the trap rock quarry sits at its southern end.

Tell the dispatcher about steep access, long driveways or gates, so the plumber arrives prepared. Branford lies to the south and Guilford, our home base, is on the east side of town.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or an active leak

North Branford has hillside houses, valley houses and neighborhoods on the town sewer, so the first minutes depend on how yours is supplied.

Shut off the pump if you are on a well

Switch off the well pump breaker and close the main valve so a broken line stops running.

Read the pressure tank gauge

If it reads zero with the pump running, tell the dispatcher. It separates a pump problem from a pipe problem.

If the basement is filling, get out of it

Do not wade through standing water to reach outlets. Unplug what you can from dry ground.

Call and say where the water goes

Tell us whether you are on septic or the town sewer, and whether a sump pump is running.
