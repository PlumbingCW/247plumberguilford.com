How to choose the right page

Start with what you see, not with what you think the fix is. Water on a floor, a drain that gurgles, a bill that jumped and a heater that went cold are all symptoms, and each service page opens with the symptoms that lead to it.

If water is running and you cannot stop it, close the main water valve, then call the 24/7 line. A plumber can sort out the right service while you are on the phone. Services marked as emergency work on the individual pages are covered by our 24/7 dispatch, and the planned services are scheduled with a plumber who can see the whole job.

The homepage covers the general emergency plumber service for Guilford and nearby towns. Individual service pages go deeper on one topic, such as hydro jetting for stubborn drain lines or water heater replacement after a tank fails.

Emergency work and planned work

Emergency work means a problem that damages property or makes the home unsafe or unusable: a burst pipe, a sewer backup, a leaking water heater, a toilet that will not stop overflowing. Planned work means a project you schedule, such as a water heater installation, a repiping project or a new bathroom.

The line answers around the clock, and the plumber who visits will explain both the cause and the price before any work begins.

Not sure which service you need?

Call the 24/7 line and describe what you see, hear or smell. If the problem is part of a larger project, the dispatcher will tell you which plumber to ask for.
